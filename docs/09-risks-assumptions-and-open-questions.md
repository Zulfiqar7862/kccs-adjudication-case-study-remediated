# Risks, Assumptions, and Open Questions

## Risks

- Incomplete stability repository may block dispositions.
- Inconsistent identifiers may cause wrong product or facility resolution.
- Timezone errors may distort reviewer timelines.
- LLM hallucination or unsupported rationale.
- Audit-log outage could compromise accountability.
- Source-system outages may delay evidence collection.

## Assumptions

- Regulatory clock is anchored to alert timestamp.
- Human approval is required for regulated disposition.
- Source systems remain systems of record.
- LLM is decision support, not decision-maker.

## Open questions

- Which tenants should be onboarded first?
- What is the authoritative source for each reference type?
- What are the exact notification obligations per market?
- What is the acceptable evidence-gap policy?
- What is the required retention period for audit history?
